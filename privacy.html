Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026.

What this website collects

When you use an order form, you may enter your name, phone number, email address, delivery or home address, chosen iPhone, colour and an optional message.

How the information is used

The website uses the details you enter to prepare your order information and open WhatsApp so you can send the message to LoversiPhones. The current website code does not use browser localStorage, sessionStorage or a server-side form endpoint to store the order form data.

WhatsApp and Google Maps

When you continue to WhatsApp, your message is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy practices. The location section uses an embedded Google Map; Google may process technical information when that map is loaded.
