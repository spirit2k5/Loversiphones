Website purpose

This site displays the current iPhone list, listed prices, storage options, colour pictures, available phone views and LoversiPhones contact information.

Orders

The site does not take payment or automatically complete a sale. The order tools prepare your details and open WhatsApp so you can send them to LoversiPhones. Live stock status is not shown on the website, so availability should be confirmed directly with LoversiPhones.

Phone-specific details

Condition, battery health, accessories, network status, warranty and return terms are not published for every phone. Confirm those details for the specific device before purchasing.

Accuracy

If you notice a listing or image that does not match the phone you are asking about, contact LoversiPhones before ordering so the current details can be confirmed.
